From discovery to decision

How to use an iTaoBuy spreadsheet

A spreadsheet is a starting point for product research: names, photos, prices and links collected into a browsable list. The listing and the purchasing service still need separate checks.

1

Find the exact listing

Choose a product and note its source ID. Open the HipobuyFind entry, inspect the underlying seller link and match the photos, model and options. Similar pictures can represent different listings.

2

Confirm the order details

If you plan to use iTaoBuy, confirm that it accepts the original seller URL. Check the selected variant, size chart, current price, domestic freight and return conditions before authorizing a purchase.

3

Review before shipping

When warehouse inspection is available, compare the received item with your order. Request measurements or closer photos where needed, then compare shipping options for your actual destination and parcel.

Beyond the sticker price

What your item price leaves out

The catalog shows product prices in Chinese yuan. A useful budget separates the goods from the costs of getting them to your door.

Cost componentWhat to checkWhen to confirm
Item and variantThe price of your chosen size, color and quantityBefore purchasing
Domestic shippingSeller-to-warehouse delivery and any seller-specific chargesBefore purchasing
Service and payment feesService charges, conversion rates and payment processingBefore payment
International freightDestination, packed dimensions, chargeable weight and route restrictionsAfter parcel details are available
Optional services and taxesPackaging, inspection extras, insurance and applicable destination chargesBefore shipping approval

Example: the ¥170 hoodie plus the ¥105 T-shirt gives a ¥275 goods subtotal. That figure is not a delivered-price quote.

Fit comes first

Check measurements

  • For tops, compare chest width, body length and sleeve length with a garment you own.
  • For footwear, check the seller’s size chart and ask for relevant measurements if sizing is unclear.
  • For bags, confirm external dimensions, closure type and included straps.
  • Do not rely on a size label or a single photo to establish fit or materials.
Look beyond the listing photo

Review QC carefully

  • Match quantity, color and variant to the order record.
  • Check visible stitching, stains, damage, fasteners and left/right consistency.
  • Ask for close-ups or measurements when a detail affects your decision.
  • Check return deadlines before approving dispatch. Photos cannot prove durability or authenticity.
Before you go

iTaoBuy spreadsheet questions

What is an iTaoBuy spreadsheet?

It is a product-discovery list used to research items before purchasing through a shopping service. This page provides a small, searchable HipobuyFind catalog snapshot and buying guidance; it is not an official agent spreadsheet or a downloadable Excel file.

Where do the buttons and product links go?

The “iTaoBuy spreadsheet” and “Buy it now” buttons both open https://hipobuyfind.com. Individual product references open their corresponding HipobuyFind pages. They do not open an iTaoBuy checkout or place an order.

Can every item here be purchased through iTaoBuy?

That has not been confirmed. Obtain the original seller link from the product entry and check acceptance, current restrictions and available options with iTaoBuy before paying. A spreadsheet topic in a listing title does not establish compatibility.

Are the prices and stock updated automatically?

No. These four entries are a dated source snapshot, not an automated inventory connection. Reopen the source listing for current variant prices and availability. The shortlist calculator uses the displayed CNY prices only.

Are these warehouse QC photos?

The images are original source-listing references. They have not been verified as warehouse QC photos. Inspect photos of the actual received goods through your purchasing service when that service is available.

Does this page guarantee quality or delivery times?

No. Product quality, seller fulfillment, returns, route availability and delivery estimates must be assessed separately. Confirm current terms with the seller and purchasing service for your order.
